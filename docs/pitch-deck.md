## 🎯 Pitch Deck Outline (9 Slides)

### Slide 1 — Title
StellarSwap+ branding, tagline, Level 5 Blue Belt badge, and key links.

### Slide 2 — Problem Statement
Four pain points in the Stellar DeFi ecosystem: fragmented DEX UX, no escrow solution, wallet fragmentation, and poor error handling.

### Slide 3 — Solution
StellarSwap+ as a unified platform: Path Payment DEX + Soroban Escrow Vault + Multi-Wallet Kit.

### Slide 4 — Market Opportunity
Stellar ecosystem metrics ($500M+ DEX volume, 8M+ accounts), and why Stellar is the right platform (3-5s finality, ~$0.00001 fees).

### Slide 5 — Architecture
Technical stack breakdown: Frontend (React/TypeScript/Vite), Smart Contracts (Soroban Rust), CI/CD (GitHub Actions), Infrastructure (Horizon + RPC).

### Slide 6 — Traction & Metrics
52+ users, 170+ transactions, 4.9/5.0 rating, 99.8% uptime. User feedback highlights and improvements made.

### Slide 7 — Growth Strategy
Six growth pillars: Community Outreach, Ecosystem Partnerships, Product-Led Growth, Data-Driven Iteration, Content & Education, Incentivized Testing.

### Slide 8 — Future Roadmap
5-quarter roadmap: Mainnet Launch → Advanced Features → Mobile & SDK → Governance → Enterprise Escrow.

Links to live app, GitHub, and feedback form.
